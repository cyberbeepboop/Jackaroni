# Jackaroni Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Jackaroni** ("the Bot", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use or interact with the Bot.

By using Jackaroni or inviting it to your Discord server, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Jackaroni may collect and store the following information to provide its functionality:

**Discord Information**
- Discord User IDs.
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
- Usernames and display names when necessary.
- Server names and configuration information.

**Moderation Information**
- Warning records.
- Ban, kick, and timeout records.
- Moderation action history.
- Moderation reasons and relevant staff information.
- Moderation command usage.

**Security Information**
- Anti-raid detection records.
- Anti-nuke detection records.
- Suspicious activity information.
- Join and leave activity when necessary for security features.
- Security configurations and protection settings.

**Technical Information**
- Bot command usage.
- Error reports and diagnostic information.
- Information required to maintain the Bot's functionality and reliability.

We only collect information reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information may be used to:

- Provide moderation functionality.
- Maintain warning and punishment records.
- Detect and prevent raids and malicious activity.
- Protect Discord servers from unauthorized actions.
- Maintain server-specific configurations.
- Investigate technical issues and errors.
- Improve the Bot's reliability, performance, and security.
- Respond to support requests.

We do not intentionally use collected information for advertising or sell personal information to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases or other secure storage systems used by Jackaroni.

Data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

Examples include:
- Moderation records retained for historical reference.
- Server configurations retained while the Bot is active in a server.
- Security logs retained for investigation and protection purposes.
- Diagnostic information retained for troubleshooting.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade collected information.

Information may only be disclosed when:
- Required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
- Necessary to protect the security of Jackaroni or its users.
- Necessary to investigate abuse, exploitation, or malicious activity.
- Required to operate services that support the Bot, where applicable.

We do not publicly expose private moderation records intentionally.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no database, hosting environment, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We cannot guarantee absolute protection against data breaches or unauthorized access.

## 6. User Data Requests

Users and server administrators may request information regarding data associated with their Discord accounts or servers.

Requests may include:
- Access to eligible stored information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion of eligible stored information.
- Removal of server-specific information.

Requests can be submitted through our official support server.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, operational, or legal purposes.

Server administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of Jackaroni complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

Server administrators are also responsible for configuring the Bot's permissions appropriately.
